# How This Content Was Made

This course is drafted with AI assistance, and read through and checked for accuracy to the best of the reviewer's current knowledge before publishing.

That review process reduces errors, it doesn't eliminate them. Treat specific facts here — numbers, version-specific details, algorithm internals — as a solid starting point for understanding a concept, not as a citation-grade source. If a detail here matters for something you're actually building or being evaluated on, verify it independently before relying on it.

If you spot something that looks wrong, it may well be — this kind of content benefits from more eyes on it, not fewer.
